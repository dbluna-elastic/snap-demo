10-Minute Demo Storyline

  1. Open the dashboard — show the map of flagged stores and top suspicious retailers.
  2. Ask the agent: "Which stores show signs of trafficking this week?" → store 4471 surfaces.
  3. Drill in: "Why is store 4471 suspicious?" → same-cent %, transaction counts.
  4. Identity fraud: "Is anyone enrolled in two states?" → cross-state match.
  5. Recipient fraud: "Show me accounts being drained to zero." → drain households at 7701.
  6. ML layer: show anomaly swimlane for store 3890 volume spike — no hand-tuned thresholds.
  7. Automation: trigger alert → workflow creates case, enriches store + recipients, notifies investigator.
